RAVANA The Final Arrow

A mixed-reality Dussehra archery battle for Meta Quest 3. Place a giant Ravana in your room, shoot his ten heads, pierce his navel and watch the effigy burn.

Starting…

    How to play
    1. Enter Mixed Reality, point a controller at your real floor and pull the trigger to place Ravana (thumbstick: height / turn).
    2. Press Start Battle on the floating panel.
    3. The hand that pulls the trigger holds the bow. Aim along the gold laser and pull the trigger to shoot. Squeeze the grip to swap hands.
    4. Destroy all ten heads. Shoot down fireballs or lean aside.

    Desktop preview: left-click to place / shoot / press buttons, right-drag to look, wheel to move, H = hitboxes, R = restart, Esc = menu.
